### ALGO-000193: Polynomial-length constant-query locally decodable codes
- **Subfield:** Quantum complexity and locally decodable codes
- **Open:** Construct constant-alphabet, constant-query locally decodable codes of polynomial block length that tolerate a constant adversarial error rate, for some fixed query count at least three.
- **Status:** Two-query codes require exponential length; constant-query constructions with much larger length and strong lower bounds leave the general polynomial-length possibility unresolved.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Would permit recovery of individual data symbols with tiny access cost and modest redundancy.
- **Key works:** Yekhanin (2007), DOI: 10.1145/1250790.1250830; Efremenko (2009), *3-Query Locally Decodable Codes of Subexponential Length*.
- **Related:** Information theory—local error correction; privacy—private information retrieval.
